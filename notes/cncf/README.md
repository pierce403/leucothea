# CNCF notes

- `INDEX.md`: generated index of CNCF-hosted projects (Graduated/Incubating/Sandbox/Archived).
- `projects/`: per-project notes generated from the CNCF Landscape snapshot.
- `stack-overview.md`: mental model for how CNCF stacks fit together.
- `security-attack-surface.md`: attack surface inventory + baseline controls.
- `day-2-ops.md`: upgrade/backup/incident-response baseline.
- `references.md`: curated “source of truth” links.

These notes are intentionally concise: they orient you to *where a project fits*, what to validate operationally, and what to consider for security/attack surface.
